How It Works

📦

1. Import

Click the bookmarklet on your Amazon library page. Your books transfer through an encrypted relay to your local browser.

📁

2. Organize

In the app, drag books into folders, tag them, sort and filter. Auto-Organize handles the heavy lifting.

📱

3. Browse Anywhere

Pair your phone with a QR code and browse your organized library on the go.
