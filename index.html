How It Works

1

Import

Add a bookmarklet to your browser, then click it. It takes you to your Amazon library page—click it again to download your books. Covers, ratings, reviews, descriptions—all transferred through an encrypted relay to your local browser.

2

Organize

In the app, drag books into folders, add tags, and sort your way. Everything stays on your device—your library, your rules.

3

Browse Anywhere

Pair your phone by scanning a QR code. Browse your library from the couch, the bookstore, or anywhere.
