    The messages — our protocol over plain HTTPS

    Everything is an ordinary HTTPS request to the worker; the response carries the data. The transport (HTTP verbs, URLs, bodies) is the universal standard — the URL shapes and payload formats below are ours alone. The worker also wakes on a clock (cron: usage-alert emails), which is why it's a “worker,” not just a listener. {ch} is your channel id — the unique UUID minted at Relay Setup that your app, fetchers, and mobile share (it also feeds the encryption-key derivation). Every payload stored is encrypted; the key stays on your devices, never in Cloudflare.
